Use the Buzzer for sound and the display as a light source.

## 🔊 Buzzer

```text
APP → Buzzer
```

| Menu | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| `Play Music` | Play a bundled sample tune. |
| `Play Note` | Play the selected note or frequency. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a sound type">
    Open `APP → Buzzer`, then select `Play Music` or `Play Note`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a sound">
    Use up/down to move through the list, then press the center button to play it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop sound">
    Press the center button to stop playback. Wait for playback to stop, then press left to go back. If the display does not respond, use [the feature-stop procedure](/en/getting-started/controls#stop-an-active-feature).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Info>
🎬 **Media to add:** A Buzzer clip showing “Choose Play Music → play → stop → choose Play Note”; keep the clip volume at a safe level.
</Info>

## 🔦 Flashlight

```text
APP → Flashlight
```

Use the screen as a light source. Follow the hint at the bottom of the display to turn it on, change its color, or turn it off. To exit, use [the feature-stop procedure](/en/getting-started/controls#stop-an-active-feature).

<Info>
🎬 **Media to add:** A Flashlight clip showing on, color change, off, and holding the center button to exit.
</Info>

<Tip>

The Flashlight uses light from the display. It is suitable for close-range illumination, not as a high-power torch.

</Tip>
