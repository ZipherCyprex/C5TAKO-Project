Use the `Bluetooth` menu to inspect or test Bluetooth Low Energy (`BLE`) devices.

<Warning>

Use only your own devices or devices you are explicitly authorized to test. `SPAM` and `AirtagTrigger` can cause numerous notifications on nearby devices.

</Warning>

## 🧭 Choose a menu

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="SPAM" icon="megaphone" href="/en/features/bluetooth-spam-airtag">
    Test BLE notifications
  </Card>
  <Card title="ATTACK" icon="radio-tower" href="/en/features/bluetooth-attack">
    Test AirtagTrigger
  </Card>
  <Card title="SNIFFER" icon="search" href="/en/features/bluetooth-sniffer">
    View nearby BLE devices
  </Card>
  <Card title="Bad BLE" icon="keyboard" href="/en/features/bluetooth-bad-ble">
    Test Bluetooth HID controls
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Note>
💡 Choose `SNIFFER` to view nearby devices. Choose `SPAM` or `ATTACK` only when testing your own device
</Note>

![Bluetooth menu on C5TAKO™](/images/device/bluetooth-menu.jpeg)

When finished, use [the feature-stop procedure](/en/getting-started/controls#stop-an-active-feature).

## If the phone does not respond

1. Confirm that Bluetooth is enabled on the phone.
2. Move the devices closer together.
3. Stop any other Bluetooth feature on C5TAKO™.
4. Turn the phone's Bluetooth off and on, then try again.
