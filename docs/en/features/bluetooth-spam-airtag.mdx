Use this page to test BLE notifications with your own phone.

Menu path: `Bluetooth → SPAM`

<Warning>

Test only in a controlled area and stop immediately when finished; nearby devices belonging to other people may receive notifications.

</Warning>

## Start and stop

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the test phone">
    Place your phone near C5TAKO™ and enable Bluetooth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a preset">
    Open `Bluetooth → SPAM` and choose the preset that matches the device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the test">
    Press the center button to start and observe the response on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Use [the feature-stop procedure](/en/getting-started/controls#stop-an-active-feature) immediately when finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>
