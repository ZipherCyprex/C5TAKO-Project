Use `AirtagTrigger` with your own phone in a controlled area.

<Warning>

Use only your own device in a controlled area, and stop as soon as the test is complete.

</Warning>

## AirtagTrigger

<Steps>
  <Step title="Prepare the test phone">
    Place your phone near C5TAKO™ and enable Bluetooth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open AirtagTrigger">
    Open `ATTACK → AirtagTrigger`, then press the center button to start.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Observe the response on your phone.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Stop">
    Use [the feature-stop procedure](/en/getting-started/controls#stop-an-active-feature) immediately when finished.
  </Step>
</Steps>
